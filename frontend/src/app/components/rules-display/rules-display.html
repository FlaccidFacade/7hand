7hand Rules

Objective: Play all 7 rounds. Be the first player to get rid of all cards in your hand by forming sets and runs.

Turn Structure

  1. Draw: Draw the top face-down card or the face-up discard (the discard may be claimed by a bid first, see Bidding)
  2. Meld the contract: (Optional) Lay down the required sets and runs from your hand
  3. Lay off: (Optional) Add cards to existing sets or runs on the table
  4. End Turn/Discard: Discard one card from your hand to the discard pile

Special Rules

  • Aces can be low (A-2-3-4) or high (J-Q-K-A), but not both in the same run
  • You must meld your contract before you can lay off cards onto melds on the table
  • The hand ends when a player has no cards left in their hand
  • Points are scored based on cards remaining in opponents' hands
  • During your discard phase you must discard down to your max hand size.

Scoring

  • Joker: 15 points each
  • Number cards (2-10): Face value
  • Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points each
  • Face cards (A): 11 points each

Contracts

Choose one available contract each hand. You cannot meld the same contract twice.

  • Contract 1: (6 cards min) Two sets of three
  • Contract 2: (7 cards min) One run + one set
  • Contract 3: (8 cards min) Two runs
  • Contract 4: (9 cards min) Three sets
  • Contract 5: (10 cards min) Two sets + one run
  • Contract 6: (11 cards min) Two runs + one set
  • Contract 7: (12 cards min) Three runs

A set is three or more cards of the same rank. A run is four or more consecutive cards of one suit. If the hand ends before you meld your contract, your easiest remaining contract is removed.

Bidding

  • You can declare intentions to bid on up to 3 cards at a time. Your declared bids show, only to you, as a stack next to the discard pile; click a bid card to remove it.
  • Each player may spend up to 3 bids per hand. During a deal, bids are replenished and declared bid intentions are cleared.
  • At the start of a turn, if the top discard matches another player's declared bid, the current player may take the card as normal or allow the bid.
  • If the bid is allowed, the bidder may confirm or withdraw it. Withdrawing cancels that declared bid and costs nothing.
  • A confirmed bid gives the bidder the face-up card plus the top face-down card from the deck, and uses one of their 3 bids for the hand.
  • If the newly revealed discard matches another declared bid, the current player faces the same choice again. The current player draws normally once no bids remain.
  • If there is a bid intention that matches the top discard, the current player may not take the top deck card as a mystery draw. They must allow the bid, or dismiss it by taking the top discard which is face up.
  • If a bid is dismissed by the bidder, the declared bid is removed and the card remains in the discard pile available for future draws, or bids. This also, gives the current player freedom to choose the mystery draw from the deck instead.
