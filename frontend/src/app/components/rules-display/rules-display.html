7hand Rules

Objective: Play all 7 rounds. Be the first player to get rid of all cards in your hand by forming sets and runs.

Turn Structure

  1. Draw: Draw a card from the deck to your hand
  2. Meld the contract: (Optional) Lay down the required sets and runs from your hand
  3. Lay off: (Optional) Add cards to existing sets or runs on the table
  4. End Turn/Discard: Discard one card from your hand to the discard pile

Special Rules

  • Aces can be low (A-2-3-4) or high (J-Q-K-A), but not both in the same run
  • You must meld your contract before you can lay off cards onto melds on the table
  • The hand ends when a player has no cards left in their hand
  • Points are scored based on cards remaining in opponents' hands
  • During your discard phase you must discard down to your max hand size./li>

Scoring

  • Joker: 15 points each
  • Number cards (2-10): Face value
  • Face cards (J, Q, K, A): 10 points each

Contracts

Choose one available contract each round. You cannot meld the same contract twice.

  • Round 1: (6 cards min) Two sets of three
  • Round 2: (7 cards min) One run + one set
  • Round 3: (8 cards min) Two runs
  • Round 4: (9 cards min) Three sets
  • Round 5: (10 cards min) Two sets + one run
  • Round 6: (11 cards min) Two runs + one set
  • Round 7: (12 cards min) Three runs

A set is three or more cards of the same rank. A run is four or more consecutive cards of one suit. If the hand ends before you meld your contract, your easiest remaining contract is removed.
